🔥 High Hypertrophy Tip: Perform this routine 4 days a week. Focus on slow eccentrics (3 seconds lowering down) to break down muscle fibers without heavy weights. Rest 60-90 seconds between sets.

Intermediate Home Hypertrophy Routine (50 Mins)

1
Decline Push-ups (Feet Elevated)
Upper Chest & Shoulders

Elevate feet on a chair or bed to shift load onto upper chest fibers and front shoulders.

Sets: 4 Reps: 12 - 15
2
Bulgarian Split Squats
Quads & Glutes (Unilateral Strength)

Place rear foot on a chair and perform deep lunges to build massive leg power and mass single-leggedly.

Sets: 4 Reps: 10 - 12 per leg
3
Underhand Inverted Towel Rows
Lats, Biceps & Upper Back
<

Use an underhand grip to maximize biceps engagement while pulling bodyweight for back width.

Sets: 4 Reps: 10 - 12
4
Elevated Feet Pike Push-ups
Deltoids & Shoulder Density

Elevate feet higher to place almost 70% of bodyweight directly onto deltoids for 3D shoulders.

Sets: 4 Reps: 8 - 10
5
Diamond Push-ups
Triceps & Inner Chest

Place hands close together in a diamond shape to isolate and overload the triceps muscles.

Sets: 3 Reps: 10 - 12
6
Single-Leg Hamstring Bridge
Hamstrings & Glute Peak

Lying on back, drive through single heel to build the back of the legs and glute thickness.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 per leg
7
Hanging/Lying Reverse Crunches
Lower Abdominal Wall

Curl pelvis upwards toward chest to stress lower abs and improve core stability.

Sets: 3 Reps: 15
