🔥 High-Intensity Alert: Perform this routine 4-5 days a week. Keep rest times short (30-45 seconds between sets) to maximize calorie burn and stamina!

Intermediate Daily Routine (40-45 Mins)

1
Burpees
Full Body Explosive Cardio

Ultimate calorie burner targeting chest, arms, core, and legs in one movement.

Sets: 4 Reps: 12 - 15
2
Jump Squats
Lower Body Power & Fat Loss

Adds explosive power to traditional squats to target glutes, quads, and hamstrings.

Sets: 4 Reps: 15 - 20
3
Decline Push-ups (or Diamond Push-ups)
Chest & Arm Strength

Elevate feet on a chair/bed to put more emphasis on the upper chest and shoulders.

Sets: 4 Reps: 12 - 15
4
Walking Lunges
Legs, Core & Balance

Strengthens the legs individually while improving core stability and balance.

Sets: 4 Reps: 12 each leg
5
Plank Shoulder Taps
Core & Shoulder Stability

Hold a high plank position while tapping opposite shoulders to engage deep core muscles.

Sets: 4 Reps: 20 total taps
6
Fast Mountain Climbers
High Speed Core Finisher

Maintain a fast rhythm to burn maximum calories at the end of your session.

Sets: 4 Duration: 45 Seconds
