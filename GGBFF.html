💡 Beginner Gym Tip: Perform this full-body routine 3 days a week (e.g., Mon/Wed/Fri). Choose light-to-moderate weights that allow proper form, and aim to progressively increase weight over time.

Beginner Gym Mass Routine (45-50 Mins)

1
Goblet Squats (Dumbbell or Kettlebell)
Quads, Glutes & Lower Body Foundation

Hold a dumbbell vertically at chest level to practice deep, controlled squatting for leg mass and shape.

Sets: 3 - 4 Reps: 10 - 12
2
Barbell / Dumbbell Glute Bridges
Glute Hypertrophy & Hip Power

Rest weight over hips while lying on a mat to heavily isolate and build glute density and shape.

Sets: 4 Reps: 12 - 15
3
Lat Pulldown (Cable Machine)
Upper Back, Lats & Hourglass Symmetry

Pull wide bar down towards upper chest to build a toned upper back and improve overall posture.

Sets: 3 Reps: 10 - 12
4
Dumbbell Flat Bench Press
Chest Firming, Front Shoulders & Core

Lie flat on a bench and press dumbbells upwards to strengthen upper body push muscles safely.

Sets: 3 Reps: 10 - 12
5
Dumbbell Seated Shoulder Press
Deltoids & Upper Body Definition

Press dumbbells overhead vertically to develop shoulder roundness and arm tone.

Sets: 3 Reps: 10 - 12
6
Cable Rope Triceps Pushdowns
Triceps & Back Arm Toning

Push cable rope downward while keeping elbows pinned to isolate and sculpt triceps muscles.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15
7
Cable / Dumbbell Biceps Curls
Biceps Peak & Arm Strength

Control weight while curling upwards to build tone and muscle definition in front arms.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15
