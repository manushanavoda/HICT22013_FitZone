💡 Quick Tip: Perform this fat-burning routine 3-4 days a week. Keep a 30-45 second rest between sets and remember to drink plenty of water!

Beginner Daily Routine (30-35 Mins)

1
Jumping Jacks
Full Body Cardio Warm-up

Great total-body warm-up to boost your heart rate and start burning calories fast.

Sets: 3 Duration: 45 Seconds
2
Bodyweight Squats
Thighs, Glutes & Lower Body

Shapes legs and glutes while engaging big muscle groups to increase calorie burn.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15
3
Knee Push-ups
Upper Body & Core Toning

A beginner-friendly variation to tone arms, chest, and shoulders comfortably.

Sets: 3 Reps: 8 - 10
4
Glute Bridges
Glutes & Hamstrings Focus

Lying flat on your back, lift hips high to strengthen glutes and lower back muscles.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15
5
Standing High Knees
Low-Impact Fat Burner

Bring knees up toward chest alternately in a quick motion to target lower belly fat.

Sets: 3 Duration: 40 Seconds
6
Forearm Plank Hold
Core Strength & Flat Stomach

Hold your body straight to build deep core strength and tighten the abdomen.

Sets: 3 Duration: 20 - 30 Seconds
