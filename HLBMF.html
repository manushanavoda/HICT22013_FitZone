💡 Quick Tip: Do this routine 3-4 days a week (e.g., Monday, Wednesday, Friday). Rest 45-60 seconds between sets. No equipment needed!

Daily Workout Routine (30-40 Mins)

1
Jumping Jacks
Warm-up & Cardio Burn

Great full-body exercise to get your heart rate up and burn calories quickly.

Sets: 3 Duration: 45 Seconds
2
Bodyweight Squats
Legs & Core

Targets quadriceps, hamstrings, and glutes to build lower body strength.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15
3
Push-ups (or Knee Push-ups)
Chest, Shoulders & Triceps

Builds upper body strength. Beginners can drop knees to the floor if needed.

Sets: 3 Reps: 8 - 12
4
High Knees
Fat Burning Cardio

Engages your core and burns abdominal fat while improving endurance.

Sets: 3 Duration: 30 Seconds
5
Plank Hold
Core & Lower Back

Strengthens the abdominal wall and stabilizes your spine and hips.

Sets: 3 Hold: 30 - 45 Seconds
6
Mountain Climbers
Full Body Fat Loss

Great finisher exercise for calorie burn and core activation.

Sets: 3 Reps: 15 each leg
