🚨 Advanced Calisthenics Alert: Perform this routine 4 days per week. Take exercises close to failure while maintaining strict form to trigger maximum hypertrophy without gym weights.

Advanced Home Mass Routine (55-60 Mins)

1
Pistol Squats (Single Leg Squat)
Unilateral Quad & Glute Hypertrophy

Puts entire bodyweight on one leg to build massive leg power, quad size, and knee stability.

Sets: 4 Reps: 8 - 10 per leg
2
Archer Push-ups
Chest & Triceps Load Shift

Shifts majority of bodyweight to one arm at a time, mimicking heavy dumbbell presses.

Sets: 4 Reps: 10 - 12 total
3
Wall-Assisted Handstand Push-ups
Maximal Deltoid Overload

The ultimate bodyweight shoulder exercise. Presses 100% of bodyweight overhead against a wall.

Sets: 4 Reps: 6 - 8
4
Single-Arm Inverted Doorway Rows
Unilateral Lats & Back Thickness

Isolates one side of the upper back at a time for deep lat contractions and biceps tension.

Sets: 4 Reps: 8 - 10 per arm
5
Triceps Bodyweight Extensions
Triceps Long-Head Isolation

Placed hands on a low table/chair and push body away using only triceps leverage.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15
6
Nordic Hamstring Curls (Door Supported)
Maximal Hamstring Hypertrophy

Anchor feet under a heavy couch/door and lower body slowly to completely isolate hamstrings.

Sets: 3 Reps: 6 - 8
7
Drag Curls (Using Resistance / Backpack)
Biceps Peak Isolation

Fill a backpack with heavy books and drag elbows back while curling to target biceps peak.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15
8
L-Sit / Dragon Flag Progression
Core Compression & Thickness

Lift legs parallel to floor while supporting body on floor/chairs to flex total abs.

Sets: 4 Hold: 15 - 20 Secs
