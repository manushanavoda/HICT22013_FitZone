The Foundation of a Healthy Life: Food & Exercise

Living a healthy life depends on two essential components: nutrition (healthy food) and physical activity (exercise).

Think of your body as a high-performance machine—healthy food acts as the high-quality fuel that keeps the engine running smoothly, while exercise keeps the mechanical parts strong, flexible, and free from wear and tear. Neither component can fully replace the other; true health requires a balance of both.
