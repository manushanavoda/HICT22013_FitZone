🔥 High Density Fat Loss Alert: Perform this routine 4 days per week (e.g., Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri). Maintain high work-capacity density with 30-45 seconds rest between sets to trigger maximum EPOC (afterburn effect) while preserving lean muscle mass!

Advanced Full Body Fat Shred Schedule (60 Mins)

1
Barbell Back Squats
Heavy Compound Movement & Maximum Caloric Burn

Squat below parallel with heavy load to trigger total-body metabolic stress and high EPOC response.

Sets: 4 Reps: 8 - 10
2
Barbell Romanian Deadlifts (RDL)
Posterior Chain Overload & Hamstrings

Hinge at hips with controlled negative lowering to maximize hamstring and glute muscle recruitment during deficit.

Sets: 4 Reps: 10 - 12
3
Standing Barbell Overhead Military Press
Shoulders, Core & Metabolic Power

Strict overhead press standing up, engaging total core stability and shoulder girdle under heavy tension.

Sets: 4 Reps: 8 - 10
4
Heavy Incline Dumbbell Chest Press
Upper Chest Hypertrophy & Power

Press heavy dumbbells on 30-degree incline to retain chest mass and shoulder definition while shedding body fat.

Sets: 4 Reps: 8 - 10
5
Bent-Over Barbell Rows (Supinated Grip)
Lats, Mid-Back & Biceps

Row heavy barbell to lower abdomen while holding a 45-degree hinge to build upper body thickness.

Sets: 4 Reps: 10 - 12
6
Superset: Cable Woodchoppers & Hanging Leg Raises
Rotational Obliques & Core Shredding

Perform cable woodchoppers back-to-back with hanging leg raises to isolate abdominal wall and keep heart rate elevated.

Sets: 3 Reps: 15 each
7
Rowing Machine or Assault Bike HIIT Intervals
High-Intensity Anaerobic Conditioning Finisher

Sprint all-out for 20 seconds followed by 40 seconds active recovery. Repeat for 10-12 total intervals.

Time: 12 Mins Interval: 20s All-Out / 40s Rest
