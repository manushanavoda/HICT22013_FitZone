Advanced Gym Routine (60-75 Mins)

1
Heavy Barbell Back Squats
Quad Dominance & Core Load

Heavy strength builder targeting maximal motor unit recruitment in the legs and core.

Sets: 5 Reps: 5
2
Conventional Barbell Deadlifts
Total Posterior Mass & Power

Pulls massive weight off the floor to stimulate central nervous system and full body muscle growth.

Sets: 4 Reps: 5 - 6
3
Flat Barbell Bench Press
Chest & Triceps Strength Peak

Pushes maximal horizontal load for building upper body pushing mass and power density.

Sets: 4 Reps: 6 - 8
4
Weighted Pull-ups
Lats & Upper Back Mass

Attach extra weight using a dip belt to maximize upper back thickness and pulling strength.

Sets: 4 Reps: 6 - 8
5
Standing Overhead Barbell Press
Deltoids & Core Stabilization

Heavy overhead pressing movement to build capped 3D shoulders and upper body power.

Sets: 4 Reps: 6 - 8
6
Weighted Chest Dips
Lower Chest & Triceps

The upper body squat. Lean forward slightly to overload lower chest and triceps heads.

Sets: 3 Reps: 8 - 10
7
Barbell Incline Cheat Curls
Biceps Hypertrophy Overload

Heavy curling variation focusing on controlled eccentric (lowering) phase for biceps destruction.

Sets: 3 Reps: 8 - 10
8
Weighted Cable Ab Crunches
Abdominal Wall Thickness

Adds heavy resistance directly to abdominal contractions to build visible blocky abs.

Sets: 4 Reps: 12 - 15
