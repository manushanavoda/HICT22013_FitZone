🚨 High Intensity Warning: Perform this routine 4-5 days per week using Tabata or Circuit style (45s work, 15s rest). Keep transition times minimal to push EPOC (Afterburn Effect) for maximum fat shredding!

Advanced MetCon Fat-Burn Routine (45-50 Mins)

1
Burpee Tuck Jumps
Explosive Cardio & Total Body Shock

Combines a full push-up burpee with an explosive knee-tuck jump to trigger maximum immediate metabolic rate.

Sets: 4 Reps: 12 - 15
2
Assisted Pistol Squats (Single Leg)
Glute Sculpting & Leg Conditioning

Isolates single-leg strength to heavily activate glutes and quads without relying on gym weights.

Sets: 4 Reps: 8 - 10 per leg
3
Plyometric Push-ups / Decline Push-ups
Upper Body Explosiveness & Arm Toning

Push off the floor explosively to tone arms, shoulders, and chest while keeping core tight.

Sets: 4 Reps: 10 - 12
4
Speed Skater Hops
Lateral Agility & Outer Glutes Burner

Dynamic side-to-side bounds that target the side hips, glutes, and burn high calories continuously.

Sets: 4 Duration: 45 Seconds
5
Commandos (Plank to Push-up)
Core Rigidity & Triceps Endurance

Transition continuously from forearm plank to full push-up position to burn arm fat and chisel abs.

Sets: 4 Reps: 12 - 16 total
6
Jump Lunges with Switch
High-Intensity Leg & Glute Burn

Switch legs explosively in mid-air to build powerful legs and accelerate fat loss.

Sets: 4 Reps: 16 total
7
V-Ups (Jackknife Crunches)
Upper & Lower Abdominal Compression

Lift legs and torso simultaneously to meet in a "V" shape, tightening the whole abdomen.

Sets: 4 Reps: 15
8
High Knees to Mountain Climber Combo
Cardio Finisher & Core Burnout

Alternate 10 standing high knees with 10 floor mountain climbers for an intense final fat-burn finish.

Sets: 3 Duration: 60 Seconds
