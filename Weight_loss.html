Weight Loss Diet Plan

Healthy meals designed to help you lose weight while keeping your body energetic, strong, and well-nourished.

Breakfast

  • ½ cup oats
  • 200 ml low-fat milk
  • 1 small banana

Lunch

  • 1 cup brown rice
  • 120 g grilled chicken breast
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables

Dinner

  • 150 g grilled fish
  • Steamed vegetables (broccoli, carrots, beans)
  • Small cucumber salad

A healthy weight loss plan helps reduce body fat while keeping your body strong and healthy. Eat more fruits, vegetables, lean protein, and whole grains. Drink plenty of water, exercise regularly, and get enough sleep. Avoid junk food, sugary drinks, and overeating for the best results.
