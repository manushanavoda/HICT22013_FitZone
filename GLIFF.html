🔥 Sculpt & Burn Tip: Perform this full-body routine 3-4 days per week (e.g., Mon/Wed/Fri/Sat). Rest 45-60 seconds between sets to maintain high intensity and calorie expenditure while sculpting legs, glutes, and core!

Intermediate Female Fat Loss Routine (50-55 Mins)

1
Barbell Hip Thrusts
Gluteus Maximus & Heavy Metabolic Burn

Drive hips upward with barbell padded across hip crease to isolate glutes, build strength, and maximize calorie burn.

Sets: 4 Reps: 10 - 12
2
Barbell or Goblet Squats
Quads, Glutes & Total Lower Body

Squat deeply with moderate weight to engage quads and glutes, pushing heart rate into high fat-burning zones.

Sets: 3 - 4 Reps: 10 - 12
3
Dumbbell Bulgarian Split Squats
Single Leg Stability, Glutes & Quads

Elevate rear foot on bench and lunge downward with dumbbells to challenge balance, tone legs, and increase workout density.

Sets: 3 Reps: 10 - 12 per leg
4
Lat Pulldown (Neutral or Wide Grip)
Lats, Back Posture & Core

Pull attachment to chest level to tone upper back muscles, supporting better posture and creating a lean waistline look.

Sets: 3 - 4 Reps: 10 - 12
5
Standing Dumbbell Shoulder Press
Shoulders, Upper Body Tone & Core

Press dumbbells overhead while standing to sculpt shoulders while forcing core muscles to brace continuously.

Sets: 3 Reps: 10 - 12
6
Cable Standing Kickbacks & Abductor Pulley
Glute Shape, Outer Thighs & Hip Tone

Use ankle cuffs on cable machine to isolate gluteus medius and maximus with constant tension.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15 per leg
7
Elliptical Machine HIIT Sprints (Cardio Finisher)
High-Intensity Fat Burning Finisher

Sprint at high resistance for 30 seconds followed by 30 seconds moderate pace recovery. Repeat for 12-15 minutes.

Time: 12 - 15 Mins Interval: 30s Sprint / 30s Rest
