💡 Muscle Building Tip: Perform this routine 3 days a week (e.g., Monday, Wednesday, Friday). Rest 60-90 seconds between sets. Focus on progressive overload by gradually increasing weight!

Beginner Gym Routine (45-60 Mins)

1
Barbell / Goblet Squats
Quadriceps, Glutes & Core

The king of lower body exercises for overall leg size and foundational core strength.

Sets: 3 Reps: 8 - 10
2
Flat Barbell Bench Press
Chest, Front Shoulders & Triceps

Primary compound exercise to build chest size, pushing power, and upper body thickness.

Sets: 3 Reps: 8 - 10
3
Lat Pulldown
Upper Back & Lats (Width)

Builds upper back width and develops the desirable V-taper physique for men.

Sets: 3 Reps: 10 - 12
4
Dumbbell Overhead Shoulder Press
Shoulders & Upper Chest

Builds shoulder width and upper body stability while seated or standing.

Sets: 3 Reps: 10 - 12
5
Dumbbell Bicep Curls
Biceps Isolation

Isolates the biceps arms to build arm peak and pulling strength.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12
6
Triceps Rope Pushdown
Triceps Isolation

Cable exercise isolating the triceps to maximize arm thickness and definition.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12
