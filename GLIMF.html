🔥 Metabolic Density Tip: Perform this full-body routine 3-4 days per week (e.g., Mon/Wed/Fri or Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri). Rest only 45-60 seconds between sets to maintain high calorie burn while preserving lean muscle mass.

Intermediate Male Fat Loss Routine (50-60 Mins)

1
Barbell Back Squats
Quads, Glutes & High Calorie Expenditure

Squat with a barbell across upper back to recruit total lower body mass, stimulating heavy metabolic demands.

Sets: 4 Reps: 10 - 12
2
Barbell / Dumbbell Romanian Deadlifts (RDL)
Hamstrings, Glutes & Posterior Chain

Hinge hips back to stretch hamstrings and engage glutes, burning energy while maintaining back strength.

Sets: 4 Reps: 10 - 12
3
Barbell Standing Overhead Press
Shoulders, Upper Chest & Core Stabilization

Press barbell overhead from collarbone level while standing, challenging shoulder power and core control.

Sets: 3 - 4 Reps: 10 - 12
4
Seated Cable Rows (Wide Grip)
Upper Back, Lats & Postural Muscles

Pull wide bar attachment to chest to build a broad upper back and increase resting metabolic rate.

Sets: 3 - 4 Reps: 10 - 12
5
Flat Dumbbell Chest Press
Chest Density, Triceps & Front Delts

Press dumbbells flat on bench with strict control to maximize pectorals engagement and muscle retention during fat loss.

Sets: 3 - 4 Reps: 10 - 12
6
Cable Woodchoppers
Obliques, Core Rotational Power & Calorie Burn

Pull cable diagonally across body with arms extended to target midsection, obliques, and elevate fat oxidation.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15 per side
7
Elliptical Machine HIIT Sprints (Cardio Finisher)
High-Intensity Fat Oxidation

Perform 30 seconds of maximum speed sprint followed by 30 seconds slow recovery. Repeat for 12-15 minutes.

Time: 12 - 15 Mins Interval: 30s Sprint / 30s Rest
