🚨 High Intensity & Hypertrophy Alert: Perform this routine 4 days per week. Take sets close to muscular failure while maintaining flawless form. Focus on maximum muscle contraction to sculpt curves without weights!

Advanced Bodyweight Muscle Plan (50-55 Mins)

1
Assisted Single-Leg Pistol Squats
Unilateral Quad & Glute Overload

Places full bodyweight onto one leg to heavily stimulate quad muscle growth and glute depth.

Sets: 4 Reps: 8 - 10 per leg
2
Single-Leg Elevated Glute Thrusts
Maximum Glute Hypertrophy & Peak

Elevate back on a couch/chair, driving through one heel to intensely shape and build the glute muscles.

Sets: 4 Reps: 12 - 15 per leg
3
Archer Push-ups (or Elevated Tempo)
Upper Body & Chest/Triceps Sculpting

Shifts body weight to one side at a time to overload chest, triceps, and front deltoids without weights.

Sets: 4 Reps: 8 - 10 per side
4
Single-Arm Inverted Doorway Rows
Unilateral Lat & Posture Building

Pulls total bodyweight using one arm to isolate back muscles, biceps, and promote hourglass symmetry.

Sets: 4 Reps: 10 - 12 per arm
5
Wall-Supported Pike Push-ups
Deltoid Density & Upper Shoulder Tone

Elevates feet high against a wall to direct bodyweight onto deltoids for round, defined shoulders.

Sets: 3 Reps: 8 - 10
6
Bodyweight Nordic Hamstring Eccentrics
Hamstring & Leg Muscle Building

Anchor feet under a heavy couch and lower upper body slowly to target posterior leg muscle growth.

Sets: 3 Reps: 6 - 8
7
Bodyweight Triceps Overhead Extensions
Triceps Long-Head Isolation

Place forearms against a firm desk or wall and bend at elbows to sculpt the back of the arms.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15
8
Hanging or Lying Dragon Flag Progressions
Total Core Compression & Tightening

Lower straight legs and torso together slowly while holding a heavy anchor to build core rigidity.

Sets: 3 Reps: 10 - 12
