🔥 Beginner Fat Burn Tip: Perform this routine 3 days per week (e.g., Mon/Wed/Fri). Focus on controlled movements with 45-60 seconds rest between sets to maintain high calorie burning while shaping tone and muscle definition!

Beginner Female Fat Burn Plan (45 Mins)

1
Dumbbell Goblet Squats
Quads, Glutes & Core Stabilization

Hold a dumbbell vertically against chest, squatting down to parallel to build legs, glutes, and burn calories safely.

Sets: 3 - 4 Reps: 12 - 15
2
Lat Pulldown (Wide Grip)
Upper Back, Posture & Waistline Visual

Pull wide bar down towards upper chest to tone back muscles, improve posture, and give an hourglass frame.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15
3
Dumbbell Romanian Deadlifts (RDL)
Hamstrings, Lower Glutes & Core

Hinge at hips keeping back flat to stretch hamstrings and firm up glutes while burning energy.

Sets: 3 - 4 Reps: 12 - 15
4
Incline Dumbbell Chest Press
Upper Chest & Front Shoulder Tone

Press light dumbbells overhead on an incline bench to firm up upper body without adding excess bulk.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15
5
Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press
Shoulder Shape & Upper Body Support

Sit back on vertical bench and press dumbbells overhead to build subtle shoulder caps that balance hip structure.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15
6
Cable Glute Kickbacks
Gluteus Maximus Isolation & Shaping

Attach ankle strap to low cable and kick backward with control to isolate glutes and tone gluteal folds.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15 per leg
7
Incline Treadmill Power Walk (Finisher)
Targeted Glute & Fat Oxidation Cardio

Walk on treadmill at 8-10% incline at speed 4.0-5.0 km/h for 15 minutes right after resistance training to burn fat directly.

Time: 15 Mins Incline: 8 - 10%
