🔥 High Intensity Hypertrophy Alert: Perform this routine 4 days per week (e.g., Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri). Train near muscular failure (1-2 RIR) with 3-second eccentric lowering phases to trigger maximum muscle growth and body shaping!

Advanced Full Body Hypertrophy Schedule (60 Mins)

1
Heavy Barbell Hip Thrusts
Maximum Glute Mass & Peak Contraction

Drive heavy barbell up with hips, squeezing glutes hard for 2 full seconds at peak contraction for maximum density.

Sets: 4 Reps: 8 - 10
2
Barbell Back Squats (Deep Range)
Quad Hypertrophy & Glute Stretch

Squat below parallel with controlled tempo to overload quadriceps and target glute-hamstring tie-in.

Sets: 4 Reps: 8 - 10
3
Deficit Dumbbell Romanian Deadlifts
Deep Hamstring Lengthening & Lower Glutes

Stand on a small weight plate for deeper range of motion to maximize hamstring hypertrophy and glute shape.

Sets: 4 Reps: 10 - 12
4
Neutral-Grip Incline Dumbbell Press
Upper Chest Sculpting & Shoulder Tie-in

Press heavy dumbbells on a 30-degree incline with palms facing inward to target upper chest without shoulder stress.

Sets: 4 Reps: 8 - 10
5
Single-Arm Neutral Cable Lat Rows
Lat Isolation & Hourglass Symmetry

Pull cable to hip with single arm to maximize lat stretch and contraction for an aesthetic v-taper look.

Sets: 3 - 4 Reps: 10 - 12 per arm
6
Cable Crossover Lateral Raises
Side Deltoid Roundness & Definition

Cross cables behind lower body to maintain continuous tension on side delts for capped, rounded shoulders.

Sets: 4 Reps: 12 - 15
7
Super-Set: Cable Katana Triceps & Incline DB Curls
Complete Arm Hypertrophy Isolation

Perform cable katana extensions for triceps back-to-back with incline bench biceps curls for maximal arm pump.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15 each
8
Weighted Decline Abdominal Crunch / Cable Kneeling Crunch
Core Thickness & Abdominal Wall Conditioning

Flex spine against cable resistance to hyper-target core hypertrophy and maintain midsection firmness.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15
