🔥 Advanced Shred Alert: Perform this routine 4 days per week (e.g., Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri). Maintain short rest intervals of 30-45 seconds to keep post-exercise oxygen consumption (EPOC) high, accelerating fat loss while maintaining muscular shape!

Advanced Female Fat Loss & Sculpt Schedule (60 Mins)

1
Heavy Barbell Hip Thrusts
Glute Overload & Maximum Energy Burn

Drive heavy barbell up through heels, pausing 1-2 seconds at full hip extension to maximize glute activation and calorie expenditure.

Sets: 4 Reps: 8 - 10
2
Barbell Back Squats
Quads, Glutes & High Caloric Expenditure

Squat to parallel or slightly below with heavy load to recruit large leg muscles, boosting resting metabolic rate.

Sets: 4 Reps: 8 - 10
3
Superset: DB Bulgarian Split Squats & Cable Glute Kickbacks
Single Leg Hypertrophy & Glute Isolation

Perform Bulgarian split squats back-to-back with standing cable kickbacks without resting between exercises for high metabolic fatigue.

Sets: 3 Reps: 10 - 12 per leg
4
Barbell / DB Romanian Deadlifts (RDL)
Hamstrings, Gluteal Fold & Lower Back

Hinge backward maintaining tight core tension to stretch hamstrings, burning body fat while improving leg definition.

Sets: 4 Reps: 10 - 12
5
Wide Grip Lat Pulldown superset DB Overhead Press
Upper Body V-Taper, Posture & Shoulders

Combine vertical pulling and pushing back-to-back to tone upper back and shoulders, accentuating a slim waist line.

Sets: 3 - 4 Reps: 10 - 12
6
Hanging Leg Raises & Cable Ab Woodchoppers
Core Conditioning & Oblique Toning

Perform hanging leg raises immediately followed by cable woodchoppers to shred lower abs and refine side waist contours.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15
7
Stairmaster / Incline Treadmill HIIT Finisher
High-Intensity Glute & Calorie Burning Finisher

Sprint or fast-pace steps for 30 seconds at max resistance/incline followed by 30 seconds slow recovery. Repeat for 12-15 minutes.

Time: 12 - 15 Mins Interval: 30s High Speed / 30s Slow
