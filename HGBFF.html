💡 Muscle Building Tip: Perform this routine 3-4 days a week. Focus on controlled movements and slow repetitions (2-3 seconds per rep) to maximize muscle contraction and firmness!

Beginner Daily Routine (35-40 Mins)

1
Bodyweight Sumo Squats
Inner Thighs, Quads & Glute Sculpting

A wide-stance squat that targets lower body muscle tone, inner thighs, and glutes.

Sets: 3 - 4 Reps: 12 - 15
2
Glute Bridge Squeeze
Glute Hypertrophy & Lower Back

Lying flat, drive through your heels and pause for 2 seconds at the top to build glute shape.

Sets: 4 Reps: 15
3
Incline / Knee Push-ups
Chest, Arms & Shoulder Firming

Builds upper body strength and tones the chest, front shoulders, and backs of the arms safely.

Sets: 3 Reps: 10 - 12
4
Doorway / Towel Rows
Upper Back & Biceps Toning

Uses home support to pull your bodyweight, strengthening the back muscles and improving posture.

Sets: 3 Reps: 10 - 12
5
Chair Triceps Dips
Triceps & Arm Muscle Tone

Targets the back of the arms to firm up sagging triceps using a steady chair or bed edge.

Sets: 3 Reps: 10 - 12
6
Bird-Dog Core Hold
Core Rigidity & Lower Back Strength

Extending opposite arm and leg engages deep core stabilizers for balance and muscle tone.

Sets: 3 Reps: 10 per side
