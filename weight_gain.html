Weight Gain Diet Plan

Healthy meals designed to help you gain weight while providing your body with energy, protein, and essential nutrients.

Breakfast

  • 1 cup oats
  • 250 ml full-fat milk
  • 2 boiled eggs
  • 1 banana

Lunch

  • 2 cups white rice
  • 150 g grilled chicken breast
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Dinner

  • 2 chapatis or 1½ cups rice
  • 150 g grilled fish
  • 1 cup vegetable curry
  • 1 cup yogurt

A healthy weight gain plan helps you gain weight in a healthy way. Eat foods rich in protein, healthy fats, whole grains, fruits, and vegetables. Have regular meals and healthy snacks, drink enough water, exercise with strength training, and get enough sleep to support healthy weight gain.
