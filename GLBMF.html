🔥 Fat Loss Tip: Perform this full-body routine 3 days per week (e.g., Mon/Wed/Fri). Keep rest intervals between sets to 45-60 seconds to keep your heart rate up and burn maximum calories!

Beginner Male Fat Burn Plan (45-50 Mins)

1
Machine Leg Press
Quads, Glutes & Heavy Calorie Burn

Engages large lower body muscle groups safely on a guided track to elevate heart rate and maximize energy expenditure.

Sets: 3 - 4 Reps: 12 - 15
2
Lat Pulldown (Wide Grip)
Upper Back, Lats & Posture

Pull wide bar smooth down to chest level to build upper back muscles, improving posture while burning calories.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15
3
Incline Dumbbell Bench Press
Chest, Upper Shoulders & Core

Press moderate dumbbells on an inclined bench to strengthen chest and arms while keeping metabolic rate high.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15
4
Seated Cable Row (Neutral Grip)
Mid Back & Biceps

Pull cable attachment toward torso while keeping back straight to activate back muscles and promote lean tissue retention.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15
5
Standing Dumbbell Overhead Press
Shoulders, Triceps & Core Balance

Press dumbbells vertically while standing to engage shoulders and force core muscles to stabilize body position.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15
6
Lying Leg Curls or Seated Hamstring Machine
Hamstring Conditioning & Knee Stability

Isolate hamstrings on machine to support full lower body development and balance quads engagement.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15
7
Incline Treadmill Walk (Post-Workout Cardio)
Low-Impact Fat Oxidation Finisher

Set treadmill incline to 8-12% at speed 3.5-4.5 km/h for 15 minutes right after resistance training to burn fat directly.

Time: 15 Mins Incline: 8 - 12%
