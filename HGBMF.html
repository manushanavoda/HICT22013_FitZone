💡 Home Muscle Building Tip: Perform this routine 3-4 days a week. Focus on controlled movements (2 seconds down, 1 second up) to maximize time under tension for muscle growth!

Beginner Home Hypertrophy Routine (40-45 Mins)

1
Standard Push-ups (or Knee Push-ups)
Chest, Triceps & Front Shoulders

The ultimate home upper body exercise to build chest density and arm strength.

Sets: 3 - 4 Reps: 8 - 12
2
Bodyweight Body Squats
Quadriceps, Glutes & Lower Body

Builds leg volume and core stability. Lower down until thighs are parallel to the floor.

Sets: 4 Reps: 15 - 20
3
Inverted Doorway/Towel Rows
Upper Back, Lats & Biceps

Uses home support to pull your bodyweight, building back thickness and biceps muscle.

Sets: 3 Reps: 10 - 12
4
Pike Push-ups
Shoulders & Upper Chest

Angles bodyweight onto shoulders to act like an overhead press for 3D shoulder growth.

Sets: 3 Reps: 8 - 10
5
Chair / Bench Dips
Triceps & Lower Chest

Isolates the back of the arms (triceps) using a stable household chair or bed edge.

Sets: 3 Reps: 10 - 12
6
Plank to Shoulder Taps
Core, Abs & Shoulder Stability

Builds core rigidity and shoulder stamina without needing any extra weights.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 16 total
