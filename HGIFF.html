🔥 Muscle Building Tip: Perform this routine 4 days a week. Focus on controlling the eccentric phase (3 seconds lowering down) and pause for 1 second at full contraction to maximize muscle hypertrophy without heavy weights!

Intermediate Muscle Building Routine (45 Mins)

1
Bulgarian Split Squats
Glutes & Quadriceps Mass

Elevating the rear foot on a chair places full focus on the front leg, heavily targeting glute shape and thighs.

Sets: 4 Reps: 10 - 12 per leg
2
Elevated Feet Glute Bridges
Maximum Glute Hypertrophy & Hamstrings

Placing heels on a chair or couch increases range of motion for deeper glute activation and strength.

Sets: 4 Reps: 12 - 15
3
Full Push-ups (or Decline Knee Push-ups)
Chest, Shoulders & Core Strength

Builds upper body definition, strengthening the chest, upper back, and shoulders effectively.

Sets: 3 - 4 Reps: 8 - 12
4
Underhand Inverted Towel / Door Rows
Upper Back & Biceps Peak

An underhand grip engages the biceps and latissimus dorsi to pull bodyweight and improve posture.

Sets: 4 Reps: 10 - 12
5
Pike Push-ups
Deltoids & Shoulder Firming

Angles bodyweight overhead to isolate the shoulder muscles (deltoids) for rounded arm tone.

Sets: 3 Reps: 8 - 10
6
Diamond Knee Push-ups / Chair Dips
Triceps & Arm Muscle Definition

Targets the back of the arms directly to build lean muscle tone and eliminate arm flab.

Sets: 3 Reps: 10 - 12
7
Side Plank Knee Drives
Obliques & Core Stability

Holding a side plank while driving top knee to chest strengthens the side waistline and deep core.

Sets: 3 Reps: 10 per side
