🚨 High Intensity Warning: Perform this routine 5 days a week. Keep rests short (20-30 seconds). Focus on explosive power and strict form to maximize caloric burn.

Advanced Daily Routine (45-50 Mins)

1
Tuck Jump Burpees
Maximum Heart Rate & Endurance

Perform a standard burpee but jump high bringing knees to chest at the top movement.

Sets: 4 Reps: 15 - 20
2
Plyometric (Jumping) Lunges
Explosive Leg Power & Fat Loss

Explode upwards from a lunge position and switch legs in mid-air before landing gracefully.

Sets: 4 Reps: 16 - 20 total
3
Clap Push-ups / Archer Push-ups
Chest & Upper Body Explosiveness

Push off the floor with enough force to clap hands in mid-air before returning to starting position.

Sets: 4 Reps: 12 - 15
4
Pike Push-ups
Shoulders & Upper Back

Elevate your hips high into a V-shape to shift bodyweight strain onto the shoulders.

Sets: 4 Reps: 12 - 15
5
V-Ups / Bicycle Crunches
Advanced Abdominal Burn

Lift torso and legs simultaneously to meet in the middle, squeezing the entire core area.

Sets: 4 Reps: 20
6
High-Speed Mountain Climbers to Plank
Core & Cardio Finisher

Sprint mountain climbers for 45 seconds followed immediately by a 15-second static plank hold.

Sets: 4 Duration: 60 Seconds
