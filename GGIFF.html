🔥 Hypertrophy Tip: Perform this full-body routine 3-4 days per week (e.g., Mon/Wed/Fri or Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri). Practice Progressive Overload by gradually increasing weight or reps each week while maintaining a 1-2 second pause at peak contraction.

Intermediate Full Body Hypertrophy Routine (50-60 Mins)

1
Barbell Hip Thrusts
Glute Max Isolation & Mass Building

Rest upper back on bench and drive loaded barbell up through heels, pausing 2 seconds at full hip extension.

Sets: 4 Reps: 10 - 12
2
Dumbbell Romanian Deadlifts (RDL)
Hamstrings & Glute-Ham Tie-in

Hinge at hips while lowering dumbbells close to shins to stretch hamstrings and build posterior chain muscle.

Sets: 4 Reps: 10 - 12
3
Seated Cable Rows (Close-Grip V-Bar)
Mid Back Thickness & Lats

Pull handle toward abdomen while squeezing shoulder blades together to build a strong, shapely back.

Sets: 3 - 4 Reps: 10 - 12
4
Incline Dumbbell Bench Press
Upper Chest Firming & Front Shoulders

Set bench to 30-degree incline and press dumbbells to lift and firm the chest upper arch.

Sets: 3 - 4 Reps: 10 - 12
5
Cable Lateral Raises
Side Deltoids & Hourglass Proportion

Maintain constant tension on side deltoids using cables to sculpt round shoulders that enhance waist symmetry.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15
6
Dumbbell Bulgarian Split Squats
Quad Muscle & Glute Hypertrophy

Hold dumbbells at sides with rear foot elevated on bench to isolate each leg for balanced muscle growth.

Sets: 3 Reps: 10 - 12 per leg
7
Overhead Cable Rope Triceps Extensions
Triceps Long Head Isolation

Extend cables overhead to fully stretch and tone the back part of arms effectively.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15
