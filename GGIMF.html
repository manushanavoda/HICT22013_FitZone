🔥 High Volume Alert: Train 3-4 days per week with alternating rest days. Maintain 60-90 seconds rest between sets and aim for progressive overload (increase weight when reps become easy).

Intermediate Gym Routine (60 Mins)

1
Barbell Deadlifts
Posterior Chain (Back, Glutes & Hamstrings)

Builds raw power and total body mass by engaging the entire back and lower body.

Sets: 4 Reps: 6 - 8
2
Incline Dumbbell Press
Upper Chest & Shoulders

Targets upper chest fullness with a deeper stretch and stabilization than barbells.

Sets: 4 Reps: 8 - 12
3
Bent-Over Barbell Rows
Back Thickness & Lats

Key exercise for creating a thick, dense back and improving posture under load.

Sets: 4 Reps: 8 - 10
4
Barbell Overhead Military Press
Deltoids & Upper Body Core

Builds broad, 3D shoulders while challenging full-body balance and stability.

Sets: 4 Reps: 8 - 10
5
Leg Press
Quadriceps & Glute Hypertrophy

Allows heavy loading on quad muscles safely without straining the lower back.

Sets: 4 Reps: 10 - 12
6
EZ-Bar Preacher Curls
Biceps Peak Isolation

Eliminates momentum to lock focus entirely on the biceps peak contraction.

Sets: 3 Reps: 10 - 12
7
Skull Crushers (Lying Triceps Ext.)
Triceps Long Head

Targets the long head of the triceps to maximize overall upper arm size.

Sets: 3 Reps: 10 - 12
8
Hanging Leg Raises
Lower Abs & Core Strength

Advanced core exercise targeting lower abdominals and hip flexors.

Sets: 3 Reps: 12 - 15
